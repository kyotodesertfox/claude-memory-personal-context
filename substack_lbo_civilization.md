## The Leveraged Buyout

A leveraged buyout is a specific financial mechanism. A buyer acquires a company using borrowed money, with the company's own assets as collateral. The debt goes on the acquired company's balance sheet. The buyer then extracts value - selling divisions, cutting costs, taking fees - and exits before the debt load collapses the host.

The host holds the bag. The buyer is already gone.

Private equity perfected this. Strip the value, load the debt, exit. The company that remains is a shell servicing obligations that were never its own. The workers, the pensioners, the customers - they inherit the aftermath. The buyer inherited the extraction.

Apply that logic one level up.

The model assumes you exit before the collapse and someone else holds the bag.

This is why the thermodynamic framing breaks down when looking for a stable end state. You are not looking at a system trying to sustain itself. You are looking at a mechanism executing extraction past the point of host viability with no stop condition built in.

The fertility crisis is not a misconfigured countermeasure. It is particle reduction - fewer future hosts entering the system. Less entropy to extract from. But the extraction continues on the existing population because the mechanism has no feedback loop that says stop.

The cultural coherence disruption is not a misconfigured countermeasure either. Coherent populations organize. Organized populations resist extraction. Scramble the coherence and the sorting becomes easier - less resistance, less coordination, clearer access to the particles.

None of these are bugs. They are features of a system that needs the host unable to organize before the LBO completes.
