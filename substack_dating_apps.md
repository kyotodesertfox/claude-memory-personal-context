# Dating Apps Are the Intake Pipeline for Divorce Court

The Hinge founder just announced Overtone - a matchmaking app with no profiles, no swiping, no browsing. The AI decides who you are compatible with.

The first response most people had was discomfort. Black Mirror. Dystopian.

I want to pull that thread. Because the discomfort is pointing at the wrong thing.

---

## The Model That Already Exists

Dating apps as they currently function have a selection mechanism. It is not stated openly, but it is visible in the architecture.

Swipe mechanics strip out context. No conversation before matching. No demonstrated values, no track record, no evidence of competence. What remains on the profile is visual presentation and the proxies for resources - job title, apartment, vacation photos, car. The things you can display. The things that signal access to money.

The algorithm surfaces profiles. The user filters on what the profile shows. The profile shows what can be displayed. What can be displayed is overwhelmingly a money signal.

This is not a coincidence of design. It is the design.

---

## Who Owns the Filter

The same institutional class that prints money built the platforms that filter for it.

When money is the selection mechanism, and you control the supply of money, you control the selection mechanism. The filter does not select for competence, character, long-term compatibility, or productive capacity. It selects for access to the currency the institution manufactures.

Competence cannot be printed. A man who builds something real, operates outside the dollar system, produces value in forms that do not photograph well - he does not surface. Not because he lost. Because the filter was never designed to find him.

The man who surfaces is the man whose life is legible to the filter. W2, apartment, title. All of it denominated in the currency the house prints for free.

---

## Churn Is the Product

Successful pair bonding would destroy the revenue model.

A couple that matches well, stays together, builds something - they exit the app and do not return. They do not generate subscription revenue. They do not re-enter the market. They are a closed loop.

The app needs open loops. It needs re-entry. It needs the match to fail at a rate that keeps users in the funnel.

This is not a bug. Churn is the product. The app is not failing at pair bonding. It is succeeding at something else - keeping users cycling through matches, subscriptions, premium tiers, and eventually back to the beginning after the relationship dissolves.

Which brings us to the next stage of the funnel.

---

## Divorce Court

The selection mechanism on dating apps pre-qualifies the extraction target for family court.

Women who filter on money signals self-select into marriages with men who have extractable assets. The court does not need to find high-value targets. The app already sorted for them.

Family court is structured around incentives that reward dissolution. No-fault divorce removes friction at the exit. Temporary restraining orders can be issued ex parte - one account, no cross-examination, legal consequences before the other party speaks. Alimony and child support are calculated on asset and income baselines established during the marriage. The longer the marriage to a high-earning man, the larger the payout.

The federal Title IV-D program pays states a percentage of child support collected. The state is a financial participant in the outcome of your divorce. It is not a neutral arbiter. It has skin in the game.

So the architecture is:

App filters for money. Marriage forms around the money signal. Dissolution triggers extraction. Court is structured to maximize the extraction. State profits from the collection. App captures the re-entry.

Dating app - divorce court - dating app.

Churn in dating. Churn in court. The loop feeds itself.

---

## What Overtone Actually Threatens

If Overtone matches on competence instead of displayed resources, it does not just disrupt dating. It disrupts the intake pipeline.

Stable pairs do not churn. They do not re-enter the app. They do not end up in family court because the match was built on something that lasts longer than a money signal. The man who builds something real but is invisible to the current filter suddenly surfaces. The woman who was running the money filter without knowing it gets matched to something her encoder was not selecting for.

That is the Black Mirror beat. Not that the technology is frightening. That it working as advertised threatens everything downstream that depends on it failing.

The question worth watching: what does Overtone charge, and who can afford it?

If there is a premium tier that improves your position in the algorithm, the money filter did not get removed. It moved upstream. The same extraction logic runs, just one stage earlier and less visible.

The pricing model will tell you whether this was built to solve the problem or built to monetize the framing of solving it.

---

## The Men the Filter Cannot See

There is one more thread worth pulling.

The men the filter does not surface are not losing the game. They are not in the game the filter is running. Invisible to the algorithm is not the same as absent from the world.

The man who operates outside the dollar system - crypto-native, barter-based, productive in ways that do not photograph well - has no profile that performs. He does not get surfaced. He does not enter the funnel. Which means he does not end up in family court. Which means the extraction mechanism cannot reach him.

The filter self-cleans. Men who would be judgment-proof never enter. Men who are extractable do. The system is not accidentally capturing the wrong people. It is filtering precisely for the right ones.

Already off the grid. They can't extract what isn't denominated in their currency.

That is not a loss. That is the exit.
